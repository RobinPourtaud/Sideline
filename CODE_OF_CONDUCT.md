# Community expectations

Sideline welcomes contributors of every background and experience level.

Be respectful and constructive. Critique code and ideas, not people. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, and sharing someone’s private information are not acceptable. Keep discussions relevant and give others room to learn.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive discussions, or restrict participation. Report conduct concerns privately to Robin at **robin@pourtaud.dev**. Reports will be handled with care and shared only as needed to address the concern.
