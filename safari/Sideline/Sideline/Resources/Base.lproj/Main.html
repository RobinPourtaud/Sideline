Open Safari Settings to enable Sideline.

  1. Enable SidelineSafari → Settings → Extensions → Sideline.
  2. Allow YouTube accessChoose “Always Allow” for www.youtube.com in website permissions.
  3. Open a videoSelect the Sideline icon in YouTube’s player controls, then Customize.
